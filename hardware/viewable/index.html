CRITICAL ERR-01 Battery Voltage Divider Disconnected from STM32 ADC
STM32.kicad_sch: (97.79, 116.84)

Root Cause & Operational Risk

The hierarchical label BATTERY_PERCENTAGE exists inside STM32.kicad_sch but is completely dangling with no wire connection. The MCU has no physical path to read the battery voltage, leaving fuel gauging inoperable.

Prescribed Engineering Solution

Route the BATTERY_PERCENTAGE hierarchical label directly to STM32 Pin 10 (PA0 / ADC1_IN0) with a 100nF filtering capacitor to GND.

Net: /Power/BATTERY_PERCENTAGE → U5.10 (PA0_10)
CRITICAL ERR-02 Status LED (D5) Unconnected to Microcontroller
STM32.kicad_sch: R20.1

Root Cause & Operational Risk

The global label STATUS_LED is attached to resistor R20 and white LED D5, but does not connect to any pin on U5. The status indicator cannot be driven by firmware.

Prescribed Engineering Solution

Connect the STATUS_LED net to STM32 Pin 18 (PB0) or Pin 29 (PA8) configured as a push-pull GPIO.

Net: STATUS_LED → U5.18 (PB0_18) [or U5.29 PA8]
CRITICAL ERR-03 WS2812B RGB Programmable LED Data Input (DIN) Floating
STM32.kicad_sch: D6 Pin 3

Root Cause & Operational Risk

Global label PROG_LED connects to D6 Pin 3 (WS2812B-2020 DIN) but terminates nowhere on the MCU. KiCad ERC flags: "Input pin not driven by any Output pins".

Prescribed Engineering Solution

Connect PROG_LED to a timer PWM/DMA capable pin: Pin 17 (PA7 / TIM3_CH2) or Pin 28 (PB15 / SPI2_MOSI) for DMA WS2812B generation.

Net: PROG_LED → U5.17 (PA7 / TIM3_CH2 / SPI1_MOSI)
CRITICAL ERR-04 LoRA Module M0 & M1 Jumpers Tied to +5V (Overvoltage Hazard)
LoRA.kicad_sch: JP1, JP5

Root Cause & Operational Risk

Solder jumpers JP1 and JP5 are connected to the +5V rail. If soldered closed, they inject 5.0V directly into Ebyte U8 pins M0 and M1. The datasheet explicitly warns: "Communication level: 3.3V. For 5V TTL, it may be at risk of burning down."

Prescribed Engineering Solution

1. Change pull-up source on JP1.2 and JP5.2 from +5V to +STM_3V3.
2. Superior Fix: Connect M0 & M1 directly to STM32 GPIOs (PB4 & PB6) so firmware can dynamically toggle Normal, Wake-on-Radio, and Deep Sleep modes.

JP1.2, JP5.2 → +STM_3V3 (3.3V) [or Route to STM32 PB4, PB6]
CRITICAL ERR-05 LoRA Transceiver AUX Handshake Pin Unconnected
LoRA.kicad_sch: U8 Pin 5

Root Cause & Operational Risk

U8 Pin 5 (AUX) is left floating. In Ebyte LoRA modules, AUX signals RF packet completion, buffer busy status, and incoming packet wake-up. Without AUX, the MCU cannot synchronize packet transmission or wake up from sleep on incoming radio messages.

Prescribed Engineering Solution

Route U8 Pin 5 (AUX) to STM32 Pin 41 (PB5 / EXTI5) with an optional 10kΩ pull-up to +STM_3V3 to support edge-triggered wake interrupts.

U8.5 (AUX) → U5.41 (PB5 / EXTI5) with 10k pull-up to +STM_3V3
HIGH ERR-06 Continuous 210 µA Parasitic Battery Drain via Voltage Divider
Power.kicad_sch: R3 (10k), R4 (10k)

Root Cause & Operational Risk

Resistors R3 (10kΩ) and R4 (10kΩ) are connected directly across the LiPo cell (+BATT to GND) upstream of power switch SW1. They draw 4.2V / 20kΩ = 210 µA 24/7. Over several months of storage, this parasitic load will deeply discharge the LiPo below 2.5V, permanently killing the cell.

Prescribed Engineering Solution

1. Connect R3 top to the switched battery rail (downstream of SW1).
2. Increase divider resistance to 1 MΩ / 1 MΩ with a 10 nF filter capacitor (reduces standby drain from 210 µA to 2.1 µA).
3. Or gate the divider ground using a small N-channel MOSFET driven by MCU GPIO.

R3 → 1MΩ (0603), R4 → 1MΩ (0603) + 10nF cap across R4
HIGH ERR-07 Battery Cannot Charge When Main Power Switch Is OFF
Power.kicad_sch: SW1 Placement

Root Cause & Operational Risk

SW1 is wired between the battery and the MCP73871 VBAT pins. When SW1 is turned OFF, the battery is physically disconnected from the charger. Plugging in USB-C powers the system (MCP73871 OUT), but the battery does NOT charge unless the switch is turned ON, forcing all components to run during charging.

Prescribed Engineering Solution

Keep the battery permanently connected to MCP73871 VBAT. Move the power switch SW1 to the MCP73871 OUT rail (between +VCOM and the TPS61023 boost converter input/enable). This allows USB charging while the device is switched OFF.

Battery → Q1 PMOS → MCP73871 VBAT. SW1 placed on +VCOM → TPS61023 VIN
HIGH ERR-08 Asymmetrical Crystal Oscillator Load Capacitors
STM32.kicad_sch: Y1 & Y2 Load Caps

Root Cause & Operational Risk

HSE 25 MHz crystal has C39=22pF and C34=33pF. LSE 32.768 kHz crystal has C31=18pF and C36=11pF. Mismatched load capacitors cause DC offset bias, frequency pulling, and high risk of crystal startup failure in cold/hot environments.

Prescribed Engineering Solution

Match both load capacitors for each crystal:
• HSE (YSX321SL-25MHz): C39 = 18 pF, C34 = 18 pF.
• LSE (YXC-Y-26): C31 = 12 pF, C36 = 12 pF.

Set C34=18pF, C39=18pF (HSE) | Set C31=12pF, C36=12pF (LSE)
MEDIUM ERR-09 Unbalanced I2C Bus Pull-Up Resistors
STM32.kicad_sch: R21 (4.7k) vs R22 (2.2k)

Root Cause & Operational Risk

I2C1 SCL has R21=4.7 kΩ while SDA has R22=2.2 kΩ. The mismatched pull-ups cause asymmetrical bus rise times ($t_r$), degrading signal integrity and causing timing violations at 400 kHz Fast-Mode.

Prescribed Engineering Solution

Standardize both I2C pull-up resistors to identical values: both 4.7 kΩ (standard) or both 2.2 kΩ (for high-speed mode).

Set R21 = 4.7 kΩ and R22 = 4.7 kΩ (0603)
MEDIUM ERR-10 Anti-Tamper Subsystem Sheet Unpopulated
Tampering.kicad_sch

Root Cause & Operational Risk

Tamper Switch → STM32 Pin 2 (PC13 / RTC_TAMP1 / WKUP2)

Intentional Design Decisions (False-Positive Guardrails)

GROUND TRUTH
ID Circuit Block Apparent Anomaly Engineering Justification (Why It Is Intentional) Classification
D-01 Dual 3.3V LDO Regulators (U2, U3) Two identical 3.3V LDOs fed from the same +5V rail. Seems redundant. Cryptographic Side-Channel Isolation: Separating the MCU digital power rail (`+STM_3V3`) from the hardware secure element (`+CRYPT_3V3`) prevents Differential Power Analysis (DPA/CPA) side-channel attacks by isolating cryptographic current signatures from digital switching transients. SECURITY ARCHITECTURE
D-02 MCP73871 Safety Timer Disabled Pin 9 (`~TE`) tied High to VBUS; ERC warns about multiple net names. Load-Sharing Operation: Because the tactical node draws active power from `OUT` during charging, the battery charges slower. Enabling the 4-6 hour safety timer would trigger false timeout aborts before the 2000 mAh battery reaches 100%. Tying `~TE` High disables the timer. LOAD SHARING INTENT
D-03 MCP73871 SEL Pin Tied High SEL held permanently High, bypassing standard 500mA USB 2.0 limit. High-Current Adapter Mode: Allows drawing up to 1.65A typical / 1.8A max from modern USB-C 5V/2A sources, supporting simultaneous peak LoRA RF transmission (~120mA+) and battery charging (~300mA) without brownout. POWER SPECIFICATION
D-04 Boost-First Power Architecture Stepping up to 5V, then stepping down to 3.3V via linear LDOs. RF Output & Analog Noise Suppression: Ebyte LoRA requires $\ge 5.0\text{V}$ for full +20 dBm RF output power. NEO-M8N active antenna performs best on 5V. Downstream linear LDOs provide >65 dB PSRR, cleaning boost switching ripple for the MCU and crypto element. RF & PSRR INTENT
D-05 WS2812B RGB LED Powered at 3.3V VDD of D6 tied to `+STM_3V3` instead of +5V. Direct 3.3V Logic Matching: WS2812B requires $V_{IH} \ge 0.7 \times V_{DD}$. Powering at 5V would require $V_{IH} \ge 3.5\text{V}$ (incompatible with direct 3.3V STM32 GPIOs). Running at 3.3V matches logic levels, eliminating external level shifter ICs. LOGIC LEVEL MATCH
D-06 Active P-MOSFET (Q1) Reverse Protection FDN304PZ P-MOS has Drain to battery (+) and Source to switch. Ultra-Low Dropout: Avoids the 0.3V - 0.7V drop of diodes. $R_{DS(on)}$ is only $36\text{ m}\Omega$, dropping less than 18 mV at 500mA. The internal body diode initiates conduction, and Gate (pulled to GND via R8) turns the channel fully ON. Reverse voltage cuts the MOSFET off. EFFICIENCY OPTIMIZATION
D-07 SE050 Standard Power Mode Pins ENA, VIN, VCC tied to `+CRYPT_3V3`, VOUT open. Autonomous I2C Wakeup: Uses software-directed Power-down mode over T=1 APDU. State, cryptographic keys, and RAM context are preserved while consuming minimal current. The chip wakes automatically on an I2C_SDA falling edge without needing extra GPIO toggles. PROTOCOL OPTIMIZATION
D-08 USB-C 5V Power-Only Sink CC1/CC2 pulled down with 5.1kΩ resistors, D+/D- floating. Dedicated Power Port: Flashing is performed via the 6-pin SWD debug header (J5). The USB-C port is intentionally configured purely as a 5V power sink with standard 5.1kΩ CC resistors. TACTICAL DESIGN

Subsystem Technical Architecture

HARDWARE MATRIX
Power Management

MCP73871-2CC (303mA charge, 4.55V VPCC) + TPS61023DRLR 5V boost (1µH inductor, 4.99V VOUT) + Dual TLV74333 LDOs for STM32 and Crypt domains.

STM32 MCU Core

STM32F411CEU6 (100MHz ARM Cortex-M4F, 512KB Flash, 128KB SRAM). 25MHz HSE crystal, 32.768kHz LSE watch crystal, 4.7µF VCAP core cap, SWD debug header.

LoRA Tactical Radio

Ebyte E32-900T20D (Semtech SX1276, 868/915MHz, +20 dBm RF output). 5V supply for maximum RF power, 3.3V UART (USART1_TX/RX), M0/M1 mode control, AUX status line.

Hardware Secure Element

NXP SE050C1HQ1 (Common Criteria EAL 6+ certified). Isolated 3.3V power domain, dedicated I2C1 bus (PB8/PB9) with 4.7kΩ pull-ups, hardware ECC/AES/SHA accelerators.

GPS Satellite Positioning

u-blox NEO-M8N GNSS module breakout. 72-channel concurrent GNSS (GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou, Galileo), 3.3V UART (USART2_TX/RX), onboard ceramic patch antenna.

Tamper Detection

Mechanical intrusion switch tied to STM32 Pin 2 (PC13 / RTC_TAMP1). Hardware tamper circuit automatically wipes RTC backup domain and executes zeroization sequence.
